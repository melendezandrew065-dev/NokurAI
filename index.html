How NokurAI Helps Students Master Any Subject

Active recall and spaced repetition are among the most effective evidence-based techniques in cognitive psychology. NokurAI pairs lecture synthesis with the SuperMemo SM-2 algorithm so you review each concept right before memory decay occurs.

  • Universal Lecture Upload: Convert PDF lecture slides, handwritten note photos, Word documents, PowerPoint decks, and audio recordings into structured flashcards.
  • Step-by-Step Topic Walkthroughs: Learn foundational principles first, verify comprehension at checkpoints, and advance to mastery.
  • Adaptive SM-2 Scheduling & Quizzes: Track daily study streaks, recall ease factors, and quiz performance across subjects including Cognitive Science, Biology, Physics, and Distributed Systems.
  • Offline Vault & Privacy: Personal study decks remain cached locally for offline review.
